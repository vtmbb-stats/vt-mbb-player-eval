Definitions
Zones
At rim: within 4 ft. Short paint: 4 to 11 ft and no more than 10 ft out from the basket. Nail or high post: within 7 ft of the middle of the floor, 10 to 17 ft out. Side: 2 ft or more off the middle of the rim.
Post-ups vs. shots
Post-up counts and PPP include every post touch, including ones that end in a pass, a foul or a turnover. The shot chart and zone numbers only include shots.
Sources
Shot locations, shot types and minutes come from ESPN play-by-play and update after every game. Post-ups by spot, PPP, share of plays and drive direction come from Synergy and update when a new export is added. 2025-26 shot types were relabeled from VT stats where ESPN only said "jumper".
